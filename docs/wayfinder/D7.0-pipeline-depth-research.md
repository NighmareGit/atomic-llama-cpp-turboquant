## Key Finding: Split Count Was Wrong

The D5.1/D6 assumption of 26-31 splits was incorrect for GPipe stage-filtered
configs. **Only 2 splits exist** per decode with D6.9 stage filtering:

| Split | Backend   | Mean total | Mean compute | Mean wait_slot | Wait/Compute |
|-------|-----------|-----------|--------------|----------------|---------------|
| 0     | RPC       | 11.4 µs   | 5.4 µs       | 0 µs           | 0x            |
| 1     | ROCm      | 9,373 µs  | 64 µs        | 9,194 µs       | **143x**      |

Split 1 spends 89.9% of wall time in `event_wait_slot` — a sync barrier waiting
for the previous decode's ROCm event. GPU compute is 64 µs; the remaining 9 ms
is synchronization waste.

## D7.1 Prototype Results — Hypothesis Disproven

A/B tested n_copies=1 vs 4 across 1-GPU and 2-GPU (RPC) configs:

| Config | GPUs | n_copies | n_max | TG | vs baseline |
|--------|------|----------|-------|-----|-------------|
| baseline | 1 | 1 | 1 | 102.5 | — |
| n_copies only | 1 | 4 | 1 | 103.3 | +0.8% |
| n_copies + nmax2 | 1 | 4 | 2 | 102.8 | +0.3% |
| RPC + nmax2 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 131.1 | +27.9% |
| RPC + nmax2 + ncp4 | 2 | 4 | 2 | **133.0** | **+29.8%** |

**n_copies has no meaningful impact.** +0.8% to +1.4% is within noise. The
hypothesis that n_copies>1 would hide the 9ms event_wait_slot was wrong.

## Why n_copies Doesn't Help GPipe

GPipe uses a different synchronization path from the non-GPipe scheduler:

```
Non-GPipe:  decode → graph_compute → pipeline_barrier() → event_wait_slot
                                                ↑
                                         n_copies helps here (copy-slot rotation)

GPipe:      decode → gpipe_wait_seq() → compute_splits() → gpipe_record_seq()
                           ↑
                    per-stage event model — n_copies has NO effect here
```

The GPipe path (`llama_decode_gpipe_multi_impl`, line 1640) calls
`ggml_sched_gpipe_wait_seq()` for per-stage event synchronization. The
`pipeline_barrier()` (line 1593) is only used in the non-GPipe graph-reuse path.
These are orthogonal mechanisms.

## Real Bottleneck: GPipe Stage-Level event_wait_slot

The 9ms `event_wait_slot` is the GPipe per-stage wait — the previous decode's
ROCm stage must complete before the current decode's ROCm stage can begin.
In single-seq GPipe, stages are sequential (stage0→stage1→stage2), so there
is no intra-decode overlap.

## Correct Mitigation: Multi-Seq (D6.5-D6.9, already built)

Multi-seq fills the pipeline with concurrent sequences:
```
seq A:  [stage0]──[stage1]──[stage2]
seq B:           [stage0]──[stage1]──[stage2]
```
Sequence B's stage0 can run while sequence A's stage1 is still in GPU compute.
This is the GPipe-equivalent of copy-slot overlap — already implemented in D6.

## Recommendation

No further investment in n_copies. Production baseline: 2-GPU RPC + n_max=2 =
**133.0 t/s TG**. For higher single-seq throughput, the bottleneck is GPU
compute (64 µs of actual work per 9ms of wait — 143:1 ratio). Multi-seq
(D6.5-D6.9) is the correct lever; it's already built.
